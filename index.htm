Welcome to the the firmware update site
for the 32cue Stepper/Sequencer and Remote Control


Instructions

  • Start by selecting the version of the hardware you have from the options below.
    IMPORTANT: The HW revision and current firmware version are displayed on the screen at boot time. Installing the incorrect firmware may have unintended consequences.

  • To update the device using WiFi:
    1. Select the download link below to download the firmware file to your mobile phone/tablet.
    2. Put the device in Firmware Update mode via the menu.
    3. Connect to the WiFi network shown on the device screen. After connecting, the firmware update page should display automatically.
      If it does not, try opening your browser and navigating to any website.
    4. Select the previously downloaded file and click install.
    5. Wait for the installation to finish. The device will automatically reboot.
    6. Verify the verison shown on the device during bootup matches the version you attempted to install.


  • To update the device by plugging it directly into your computer:
    1. IMPORTANT: Be sure to use a USB-C cable capable of both data and power. If you attempt the below and don't see the device in step 4 below, try using a different cable.
    2. IMPORTANT: TURN OFF YOUR DEVICE before plugging it into the computer to update. It must remain off for the duration of the update process.
    3. Prior to plugging in your device, click the Connect button below. A list with available COM ports will apear.
      Doing this first makes it easier to identify which port your device is connected to.
    4. Plug in your device to a USB port on your computer (you should only have one device connected at a time).
      Connect to the port that appears in the list.
      If no new port appears, disconnect the device, hold down the knob on the stepper/sequencer, and reconnect.
      Do not release the knob until the update process has begun.
    5. Press Install and wait for the installation to finish.
    6. DISCONNECT THE DEVICE FROM YOUR COMPUTER.
    7. Turn on your device and verify the verison shown on the device during bootup matches the version you attempted to install.
